## 2. AI and Technology

## AI agents are becoming production systems

### Confirmed facts
- Companies are moving AI from chat interfaces toward agents capable of using tools, calling APIs, executing workflows, and interacting with enterprise data.
- Major challenges remain around permissions, reliability, evaluation, and cost control.

### Why it matters
Agent systems create a new engineering layer similar to distributed systems:
- state management
- retries
- observability
- security boundaries
- human approval flows

### Practical impact for developers
Build reusable components:
- Agent orchestration layer
- Tool permission framework
- Evaluation pipelines
- Prompt/version management
- AI telemetry dashboards

### Risks
- Excessive permissions
- Uncontrolled API/tool execution
- Hidden operational costs

## 2. Executive Review

Day-002 có core concept chính xác: audio và transcript được phân biệt đúng; first attempt được lưu trước support; replay có mục tiêu; transcript được dùng sau response rồi learner quay lại audio. Lesson bám đúng Phase 0, không dạy grammar, pronunciation system, TOEIC item hay score trước lịch. Exercise progression nhìn chung đầy đủ, mọi answer-bearing block đều có lời giải, và không phát hiện wrong answer key.

Tuy nhiên lesson chưa an toàn để publish. Hai rehearsal Listening chính tự tiết lộ token cần nghe ngay trong heading và câu dẫn trước first listen: `replay` ở Phần 10 và `pause` ở Phần 16. Transcript nằm trong `<details>`, nhưng answer đã bị lộ ở bên ngoài, nên các response trong hai activity không tạo được valid Listening evidence. Lỗi này ảnh hưởng trực tiếp blueprint requirement “no answer leaked by heading” và mastery claims dựa trên hai rehearsals.

Ngoài ra, lesson dùng dày đặc English meta-language không thuộc target vocabulary—`response`, `evidence`, `support`, `token`, `target`, `protocol`, `observation`, `verified`, `unavailable`, `technical failure`, `language observation`, `match/mismatch`, `flow`, `reveal`—trong learner-facing core instructions. Với learner Zero→Pre-A1, đây là hidden vocabulary burden có tính hệ thống và tái phạm đúng quality risk đã được review Day-001 chỉ ra.

Lesson có nền tảng tốt và có thể sửa được mà không redesign curriculum, nhưng phải sửa và revalidate trước khi publish.

## 6. Critical Issues

### CRITICAL-001 — Listening answers are exposed before the attempt

**Location:**

- Phần 10 heading: `Luyện có hướng dẫn 1 — Nghe replay`.
- Phần 10 immediate cue: “Trước khi bắt đầu… `replay` = phát lại/nghe lại.”
- Phần 10 Step 2 asks learner to decide between `replay`, `pause`, uncertain, or not recognized.
- Phần 16 heading: `Luyện có ít gợi ý hơn — Nghe pause`.
- Phần 16 immediate cue: “Bạn đã biết: `pause` = tạm dừng.”
- Phần 16 then asks learner to record `pause` / `replay` / uncertain / not recognized.

**Problem:** The exact spoken token is visible before first listen in both principal learner-executed rehearsals. Although transcript blocks are collapsed, the answer has already been disclosed by the heading and cue. A learner can select the keyed token without processing audio.

**Why This Is Critical:** These are answer-bearing Listening tasks used as evidence for first-attempt recording, label recognition, scaffold fading and mastery. The task construct is invalid: response correctness cannot be attributed to listening. The blueprint explicitly prohibits answer leakage through heading, filename, transcript summary or nearby text. Publishing the lesson would create false Listening evidence.

**Affected Content:**

- Phần 10 full rehearsal, transcript analysis and feedback.
- Phần 16 reduced-support rehearsal, transcript analysis and feedback.
- Phần 17 independent practice because its pool consists of previously disclosed mappings.
- Phần 21 Quiz Câu 11 when it reuses the three already exposed assets.
- Phần 23 Strong/Partial/Weak mastery interpretation.
- Phần 27 checklist items claiming independent listening protocol evidence.
- Phần 31 state-evidence fields for label recognition and protocol independence.

**Required Fix:**

1. Rename rehearsal headings and asset references with neutral identifiers that do not reveal the token, for example `Listening Rehearsal A` and `Listening Rehearsal B`.
2. Teach the functions of both candidate labels in a separate vocabulary section before the task, but do not state which one the upcoming asset contains.
3. Remove nearby sentences that identify the upcoming token.
4. Ensure learner must discriminate among already taught labels based on audio alone.
5. Provide at least one neutral, not-yet-revealed asset for independent/mastery use, or explicitly limit the independent check to process behavior and stop claiming fresh spoken-label recognition.
6. Revalidate the transcript–asset mapping, response options, feedback, quiz, checklist and mastery language after the asset/task restructuring.

**Validation After Fix:**

- Search all pre-attempt text for the canonical token and confirm it does not appear in the heading, cue, filename, alt text or instructions.
- Have an independent reviewer attempt each task without audio; the keyed token must not be inferable from page text.
- Confirm transcript remains collapsed until after a committed response.
- Confirm independent/mastery evidence uses a token whose mapping has not been exposed immediately beforehand.

## 7. Major Issues

### MAJOR-001 — Pervasive English meta-language creates a hidden prerequisite for a zero learner

**Location:** Throughout learner-facing prose, especially Phần 1–8, 10, 14–21, 23–31. Representative terms include:

- `response` (used repeatedly in core instructions and mastery fields);
- `evidence`, `support`, `token`, `target`, `protocol`, `flow`;
- `observation`, `recognition`, `representation`;
- `verified`, `unavailable`, `technical failure`, `language observation`;
- `match/mismatch`, `reveal`, `playback`, `asset`, `process issue`, `access issue`;
- `Strong mastery`, `Partial mastery`, `Weak mastery`.

**Problem:** Most of these are neither approved target vocabulary nor necessary stable course labels. They are often used as if self-explanatory inside instructions the learner must follow. The density is systemic rather than isolated: the lesson uses dozens of occurrences of `response`, `token`, `target`, `evidence`, `support` and related terms.

**Why This Is Major:** The default learner is Zero→Pre-A1, English knowledge is not established, and the blueprint explicitly requires Vietnamese-first wording while warning against the same hidden meta-vocabulary found in Day-001 review. A learner can understand the surrounding Vietnamese only by inferring many English nouns/adjectives central to the procedure. This adds avoidable extraneous load and directly violates the no-unexplained-knowledge and language-policy requirements.

**Affected Content:**

- Core flow explanation and all seven steps.
- Technical/access reporting.
- Guided and reduced-support rehearsals.
- Error correction and answer explanations.
- Independent practice record.
- Quiz and challenge.
- Mastery/remediation labels.
- Checklist, homework and state-evidence table.

**Required Fix:**

Use natural Vietnamese consistently for non-target meta-language. Recommended replacements include:

- `response` → câu trả lời/ghi nhận;
- `evidence` → bằng chứng/dữ liệu ban đầu;
- `support` → phần hỗ trợ/sự hỗ trợ;
- `token` → từ hoặc nhãn ngắn;
- `target` → mục tiêu;
- `protocol`/`flow` → quy trình/thứ tự bước;
- `observation` → ghi nhận;
- `recognition` → khả năng nhận ra;
- `representation` → dạng thể hiện;
- `verified`/`unavailable`/`technical failure` → đã xác thực/chưa có/lỗi kỹ thuật;
- `language observation` → ghi nhận về việc nghe tiếng Anh;
- `match/mismatch` → khớp/không khớp;
- `reveal` → mở phần chữ;
- `asset` → tệp/tài sản âm thanh, with one short production-facing label only where needed;
- `Strong/Partial/Weak mastery` → Nắm vững tốt/Nắm một phần/Nền tảng còn yếu, with English omitted or clearly secondary.

Retain English only for the approved functional targets `listen`, `pause`, `replay`, `transcript`, the already established course label `Learn Mode`, and `Listening` where it has already been contextualized. If a production-only technical field must remain English, separate it visually from learner instructions and gloss it once.

**Validation After Fix:**

- Search learner-facing prose for unexplained English words outside the approved set.
- Confirm every required action can be followed using Vietnamese alone.
- Confirm target vocabulary remains exactly the planned functional labels rather than silently expanding.
- Recheck that Vietnamese replacements do not alter answer references or table meanings.

## 8. Minor Issues

### MINOR-001 — Language self-report options overlap instead of separating guess and certainty

**Location:**

- Phần 5.2 language observation checkboxes.
- Phần 10 Step 2: “Tôi nghĩ mình nghe `replay`/`pause`”, “Tôi chưa chắc”, “Tôi chưa nhận ra”.
- Phần 16 Step 3: `pause` / `replay` / `chưa chắc` / `chưa nhận ra`.
- Phần 17 independent record combines recognition state and free guess without a fixed relation.

**Problem:** A learner can reasonably think the token is `replay` and also be “chưa chắc”. The options mix two dimensions: content guess and confidence/recognition status. Where the lesson asks for one selection, more than one statement may be simultaneously true.

**Why This Is Minor:** These are formative self-reports, not keyed MCQs, and later sections sometimes add a separate confidence field. Core teaching remains usable. However the record is not clean enough for the evidence contract in the blueprint.

**Affected Content:** First-attempt records in both rehearsals, independent practice table, mastery and later state interpretation.

**Required Fix:** Separate into two fields:

1. **Phỏng đoán nội dung:** `listen` / `pause` / `replay` / chưa có phỏng đoán.
2. **Mức chắc chắn:** chắc / chưa chắc; or a mutually exclusive recognition status: nhận ra / có phỏng đoán nhưng chưa chắc / chưa nhận ra.

Use the same schema in guided, reduced-support and independent records.

**Validation After Fix:** Verify that each field represents one dimension, its options are mutually exclusive, and no learner is forced to discard a valid “guess + uncertain” combination.

### MINOR-002 — Approved bounded future preview is incomplete

**Location:** Phần 3 boundary list, Phần 28 summary, Phần 32 Next Lesson Preview.

**Problem:** The blueprint explicitly identifies future preview targets—sound–text mapping, dictation, shadowing, listening for gist/detail, and TOEIC Parts 1–4—and requires them to be labeled as future/not required today. The lesson implies future Listening use and mentions TOEIC broadly, but does not provide the approved bounded preview or clarify these later techniques are not current mastery targets.

**Why This Is Minor:** The omission does not prevent learner mastery of Day-002 and may even reduce immediate load. It is nevertheless a specific blueprint continuity requirement.

**Affected Content:** Lesson overview/boundary and future connection.

**Required Fix:** Add one short Vietnamese-led future-preview note stating that later lessons will use this routine for matching sound to text, listening for main idea/detail, short dictation, optional shadowing and eventual TOEIC Listening Parts 1–4; explicitly say none must be learned today. Gloss or avoid technical English terms.

**Validation After Fix:** Confirm the preview is brief, non-assessed, beginner-accessible and does not expand into actual pronunciation, dictation, shadowing or TOEIC instruction.
